
## 4.4. Confirmation on fourteen further instruments

The batch results above rest on four settings, and the planning model was formulated after seeing them. Both were therefore put to a test on data that had not been touched: {{r15_n}} combinations of handheld unit and season, each with its own consignments, an original calibration built without any of their fruit, and slope/bias correction as the transfer. The predictions and pass criteria were written down before the analysis (protocol, section E15). The transfers worked well (RMSEP {{r15_rmsep}}% dry matter with 10 × 2 standards), and errors were again shared within consignments (intra-consignment correlation {{r15_icc}}).

The batch effect replicated without exception (Fig. 5a; Supplementary Table S15). With 20 standards from two consignments, the sample-level intervals were too narrow for new consignments in all fourteen settings: TJ+N in {{r15_tjn_K2}} (coverage {{r15_tjn_K2_cov}}), the Gaussian interval in {{r15_gauss_K2}} and the textbook regression interval of slope/bias correction in {{r15_ols_K2}}. With five consignments they still failed in {{r15_tjn_K5}} settings (coverage {{r15_tjn_K5_cov}}), and with ten in about half ({{r15_tjn_K10}} for TJ+N). With one fruit from each of 20 consignments, possible in two settings, none was too narrow. The intervals that leave out whole consignments were never too narrow: TJ+B in {{r15_tjb_under}} cells where it was bounded, and the Gaussian and random-effects consignment formulas in {{r15_lgo_anova_under}} cells with five or more consignments. The random-effects interval was valid even with two consignments, but then {{r15_anova_width_k2}} times wider than TJ+N.

The planning model passed its test with one exception. Using each setting's observed intra-consignment correlation, it predicted the coverage of the sample-level intervals with a mean absolute error of {{r15_mae_tjn}} (TJ+N) and {{r15_mae_gauss}} (Gaussian), within the pre-set limit of 0.04, and with a correlation of {{r15_corr}} between predicted and observed values (Fig. 5b). The errors were not centred: the model was optimistic by {{r15_bias}} on average, most of all for two consignments. With a single prior correlation of 0.5 for every setting, the predictions for five or more consignments were within {{r15_prior_maxdiff_k5}} of the observed mean coverage. For two consignments the observed coverage ({{r15_prior_k2_obs}}) fell {{r15_prior_maxdiff}} below the prediction ({{r15_prior_k2_pred}}), outside the pre-set ±0.05. For planning, the model should therefore be read as an upper bound on what few batches can deliver.
