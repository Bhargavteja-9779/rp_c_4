# Rejection Simulation Board and Rejection Risk Register (Phase 3)

## How the board was run

**Disclosure.** Truly independent reviewers were not available. The ten roles below are **simulated review passes by the same model**. One exception is the prior-art investigation (Reviewer C), which ran as a separate agent with its own searches, verified through Crossref and arXiv.

To avoid repetitive lists, each pass was given a single failure mode and had to produce **evidence from files or new computations**, not opinions. Issues already fixed by the first office were not re-listed unless the fix was inadequate.

## Board passes (one failure mode each)

| Role | Failure mode investigated | Main finding (evidence) |
|---|---|---|
| A. Desk-reject editor | Significance and novelty at first glance | The abstract leads with "TJ+N never under-covers". A statistically literate editor recognises this as guaranteed by Barber et al. Thm. 1 under the random-standards design. The paper reads as "known method applied", so desk-rejection risk is real (R01). |
| B. Leading chemometrician | Does it matter beyond this implementation? | Real transfer standards come from few lots, are often chosen by design, and are measured at standardisation time. Every reported validity result assumes random standards from the routine population (R02). Laboratories validate with β-expectation tolerance intervals (accuracy profiles), and the paper does not speak that language (R08). |
| C. Prior-art investigator | Already known? | Leave-one-group-out jackknife+ with a 1−2α guarantee for a new group exists (Lee, Barber & Willett 2026), so any batch extension must be framed as an instantiation (R03). The 2026 CILS review by Haggam et al. names uncertainty-aware validation as a gap, which supports the topic. Duma et al. is now published (citation outdated). |
| D. Experimental methodologist | Design, splits, baselines | No leakage (see integrity report). The design is structurally optimistic: standards and test samples are drawn from the same pool, so they are exchangeable by construction (R02). Baselines that a chemometrician would use first are missing: OLS-PI for SBC, bootstrap, ASTM re-estimated on the standards, and random-effects intervals (R04). |
| E. Statistician | Inference validity | "Not flagged" is absence of evidence, and power is not stated per cell (R05). Scenario dependence is handled only descriptively (R06). Realised coverage for a given set of standards can be 0.70–0.81 at m = 20 (5th percentile), and no training-conditional statement is possible for jackknife+ in general (Bian & Barber 2023) (R07). |
| F. Dataset / generalisation auditor | External validity | All three data sets are public benchmarks. The mango data contain lot (population) and harvest-date structure that was ignored. No prospective (time-ordered) evaluation was done (R02, R09). Only one data source has two shift types (season and handheld instruments), so the evidence is concentrated on mango (R10). |
| G. Competing group | Alternative explanations | (i) TJ+N's validity is explained entirely by exchangeability, not by anything specific to the method (R01). (ii) Its advantage over Gauss-LOO at 95% might reflect small-m t-quantile miscalibration or heavy tails; the mechanism is untested (R11). (iii) The normalisation gain of 2–6% is within what a tuned Gaussian interval achieves (Gauss-LOO-N is 3% narrower) (R12). (iv) Hyperparameters: the PDS window was not tuned, but all methods share the predictor, so no unfair advantage. |
| H. Reproducibility engineer | Can it be reproduced? | 64/64 raw-file hashes match. 175/175 manuscript values regenerate exactly. 11/11 original tests pass. A full re-execution was not repeated (4–5 CPU-h), so the original fresh-clone audit stands (R13, low). |
| I. ACA associate editor | Journal fit and requirements | Scope fit is acceptable if framed as analytical measurement uncertainty. The 72-day constraint is unverifiable for one manuscript: 67.8% of accepted ACA papers fall within it, 90th percentile 103 d (R14). Official guide pages could not be fetched (HTTP 403) (R15). The manuscript is 9,100 words with 9 figures, which is long (R16). |
| J. Final rejection committee | Strongest combined case | "The guarantee is borrowed. The experiments confirm it only where it is guaranteed. The practically relevant regime (standards from a few lots, future lots, drift) is either untested or shown to fail. Where it holds, simple parametric intervals do as well." This combines R01 + R02 + R04 and is the case the reinforcement must answer. |
